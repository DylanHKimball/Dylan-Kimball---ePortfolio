                                                      FARKLE RULES
________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
Farkle is a dice game that is multi-player with a minimum of two players, but no upper limit on the number of participants

The goal is to be the first player to reach 10,000 points
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - POINTS:

 * A roll of 1 is worth 100 points.
 * A roll of 5 is worth 50 points.
 * Three dice rolled at the same time with the same value is worth 100 times that face value.
     - For example, three 2's rolles is worth 200 points and Three 5's is worth 500 points
     - One exception is that three 1's is worth 1000 points instead of 100
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - GAMEPLAY:

 * To enter the game, each player must continue to roll until they get 1,000 points in a single turn.
 * If player has entered the game, they can decide to either stop and keep their points, or continue to roll 
     the remaining dice.
 * The first player rolls all six dice at once and sets aside any point dice 
     - Only one point dice must be set aside, players have the option to re roll the rest
 * If a player decides to not roll, then their collected points for that turn are stored and their turn ends
 * A Farkle occurs when the dice are rolled and no point dice appear. When this happens the player looses all their collected 
     point dice for that turn and their turn ends.
 * If all six dice become point dice in one turn, the the player must roll all six dice at least one more time before stopping 
     and storing their points.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - WINNING:

*In order to win, a player must reach 10,000 points.
*Once a player reaches 10,000 points, all other players get one last turn to try and beat this player.
     - If someone does get a higher score than this player then they become the winner.
     - If no player beats their score then the first player that went out is the winner.
